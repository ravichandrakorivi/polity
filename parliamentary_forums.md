# Parliamentary Forums in India

## 1. Meaning and objectives

Parliamentary Forums provide MPs a platform to discuss important public issues with ministers, officials, experts and other stakeholders. They aim to improve MPs' awareness, provide technical inputs and facilitate informed discussion in Parliament and its committees.

- They are **not the same as Parliamentary Committees**.
- They do **not** replace or encroach on the jurisdiction of Department-related Standing Committees (DRSCs).
- They do not enact laws, approve Demands for Grants or exercise the regular scrutiny functions of parliamentary committees.
- They may compile and circulate relevant information for members.

## 2. Establishment

The first Parliamentary Forum, **on Water Conservation and Management**, was established in **2005**. Eight forums were established in the historical framework:

| Forum | Year |
|---|---|
| Water Conservation and Management | 2005 |
| Youth | 2006 |
| Children | 2006 |
| Population and Public Health | 2006 |
| Global Warming and Climate Change | 2008 |
| Disaster Management | 2011 |
| Artisans and Craftspeople | 2013 |
| Millennium Development Goals | 2013 |

**Note:** This is the historically established list, not a verified statement that all eight operate unchanged in October 2026. The MDGs were succeeded globally by the SDGs in 2015; do not retroactively rename the forum established in 2013.

## 3. Composition and organisation

- **President:** Speaker of Lok Sabha is ex officio President of the Forums **except** the Forum on Population and Public Health.
- **Exception:** The **Chairman of Rajya Sabha** is ex officio President of the Forum on Population and Public Health; the **Speaker of Lok Sabha** is its ex officio Co-President.
- **Vice-Presidents:** Depending on the forum, ex officio Vice-Presidents include the Deputy Speaker of Lok Sabha, Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha, concerned Union Ministers and chairpersons of relevant DRSCs.
- **Ordinary membership:** Maximum **31**, excluding the President, Co-President and Vice-Presidents: up to **21 Lok Sabha** and **10 Rajya Sabha** members.
- **Selection:** Members are nominated by the respective presiding officers from MPs with relevant interest or expertise.
- **Tenure:** Co-terminus with their membership of the respective House, rather than a fixed annual term.
- **Secretary:** Secretary-General of Lok Sabha serves as Secretary to the Forums under the traditional arrangements.

## 4. Parliamentary Forums versus Parliamentary Committees

| Parliamentary Forums | Parliamentary Committees |
|---|---|
| Discussion, awareness and expert interaction | Formal scrutiny, examination and oversight |
| Do not ordinarily scrutinise Bills or accounts | May examine Bills, expenditure and administration |
| Do not replace DRSCs | DRSCs have defined oversight responsibilities |
| Provide a platform for MPs to exchange views | Present reports and recommendations under parliamentary rules |

## 5. Prelims revision points

1. First Forum: **Water Conservation and Management (2005)**.
2. Traditional total: **eight Forums**, established between 2005 and 2013.
3. **Population and Public Health** is the exception to the Speaker-as-President rule.
4. Maximum ordinary membership: **31 (21 LS + 10 RS)**.
5. Membership is **nominated**, not elected by STV.
6. Membership tenure is **co-terminus with House membership**.
7. Forums **cannot encroach on DRSC jurisdiction**.
8. **Millennium Development Goals** is the original historical forum name, not Sustainable Development Goals.

## UPSC CSE Prelims Practice Questions: Parliamentary Forums

### Q1. Consider the following statements:

1. Parliamentary Forums facilitate interactions of MPs with ministers, experts and officials.
2. Parliamentary Forums have the power to approve Demands for Grants.
3. Parliamentary Forums are intended to supplement, not replace, Department-related Standing Committees.

Which statements are correct?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2 and 3
</div>

<br>

**Answer: C**

> **Explanation:** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Forums facilitate discussion and must not encroach on DRSC jurisdiction. They do not approve Demands for Grants.

### Q2. The first Parliamentary Forum established in India concerned which subject?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> Water Conservation and Management<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Population and Public Health<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Disaster Management<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Global Warming and Climate Change
</div>

<br>

**Answer: A**

> **Explanation:** The first Parliamentary Forum was established in 2005 on Water Conservation and Management.

### Q3. Which one of the following Parliamentary Forums has the Chairman of Rajya Sabha as its ex officio President under the traditional framework?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> Forum on Youth<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Forum on Children<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Forum on Disaster Management<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Forum on Population and Public Health
</div>

<br>

**Answer: D**

> **Explanation:** This is the exception to the usual arrangement: the Chairman of Rajya Sabha is President and the Lok Sabha Speaker is Co-President.

### Q4. Consider the following statements about ordinary membership of Parliamentary Forums:

1. Each Forum may have up to 31 ordinary members, excluding ex officio office-bearers.
2. Up to 21 ordinary members may be from Lok Sabha and 10 from Rajya Sabha.
3. Members are elected by proportional representation using the single transferable vote.

Which statements are correct?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2 and 3
</div>

<br>

**Answer: B**

> **Explanation:** Statements 1 and 2 describe the traditional membership ceiling. Members are nominated by the respective presiding officers, not elected by STV.

### Q5. Which statement best distinguishes Parliamentary Forums from Parliamentary Committees?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> Forums are judicial bodies, while Committees are legislative bodies.<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Forums mainly promote informed interaction; Committees perform formal scrutiny and oversight.<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Forums alone can recommend changes to Bills, while Committees cannot.<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Forums vote on Budget grants, while Committees enact legislation.
</div>

<br>

**Answer: B**

> **Explanation:** Forums are discussion and knowledge-sharing platforms. Parliamentary Committees may examine Bills, expenditure and administration and submit formal reports.

### Q6. Consider the following statements:

1. The membership tenure of a Parliamentary Forum is ordinarily co-terminus with the member's membership in the respective House.
2. Every Parliamentary Forum is newly constituted at the beginning of each Budget Session.
3. The Secretary-General of Lok Sabha serves as Secretary to the Forums under the traditional framework.

Which statements are correct?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2 and 3
</div>

<br>

**Answer: A**

> **Explanation:** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. The Forums are not automatically newly constituted at the beginning of every Budget Session.

### Q7. Consider the following statements:

1. Parliamentary Forums may enable MPs to interact with specialised national and international experts.
2. Parliamentary Forums may help MPs raise informed issues during House proceedings.
3. Parliamentary Forums can issue binding directions to DRSCs on matters within the latter's jurisdiction.

Which statements are correct?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 1, 2 and 3<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1 and 2 only
</div>

<br>

**Answer: D**

> **Explanation:** Forums support informed parliamentary participation, but they cannot direct or override DRSCs.

### Q8. Assertion (A): Parliamentary Forums are intended to improve the ability of MPs to discuss specialised public-policy issues.

### Reason (R): Parliamentary Forums provide opportunities for interaction with experts, concerned ministers and officials.

Select the correct answer.

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> Both A and R are correct, and R correctly explains A.<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Both A and R are correct, but R does not explain A.<br>
<strong>C.</strong> A is correct, but R is incorrect.<br>
<strong>D.</strong> A is incorrect, but R is correct.
</div>

<br>

**Answer: A**

> **Explanation:** Both statements are correct. Expert and official interaction is one of the means by which Forums improve MPs' understanding of specialised issues.

### Q9. Assertion (A): The Parliamentary Forum on Millennium Development Goals was established after the United Nations adopted the Sustainable Development Goals in 2015.

### Reason (R): The Parliamentary Forum on Millennium Development Goals was established in 2013.
